# NOVA catalog source review

The complete draft import is transcribed from the visible product tables on pages 5–20 of `catalog 2024.pdf`. The records remain unpublished because the source omits commercial data.

## Source issues preserved for owner review

- The cover says **Catalog 2024**, while internal footers and PDF metadata say **Catalog 2023**.
- `HCN-3050` appears twice: once for a 3 mm-high / 5.0 mm-diameter healing abutment and once for a 5 mm-high / 3.8 mm-diameter healing abutment. Both source rows are preserved as drafts and tagged `catalog-review`.
- The PSI and PCI 6.0 mm implant tables show an 11.5 mm length but print `L11.6` in the corresponding catalog numbers. The Shopify SKU is preserved as printed; the displayed length remains 11.5 mm.
- `ST-1113` is paired with a displayed length of 12 mm in the catalog.
- The surgical-kit list includes a drill extension without a catalog number. It is not imported as a sellable variant.
- The current catalog does not supply price, packed weight, stock, barcode/GTIN, or ecommerce-ready individual product photography.
- The supplied IFU header says `TF-1008, revision 8`, while its last-page footer says `TF-1006/Ver.08/27.04.2021`. It remains unpublished pending controlled-copy confirmation.

## Import safeguards

- Every generated product is `Draft` and unpublished from the Online Store.
- Placeholder price is `0.00`; it must be replaced before activation.
- Inventory tracking and quantities are not set.
- Shipping weight, tax treatment and fulfillment configuration are not asserted.
- Compatibility and clinical use must be confirmed against current labeling and the controlled IFU.
